## Reproduction boundaries

All figure-source files are public aggregates. Restricted inputs are needed to regenerate their analysis values; they are not made available by a plotting command. Synthetic entry points and any remaining restrictions are identified in synthetic/README.md. Only fixed true-sample assertions may be disabled in synthetic mode; calculations, filters and decision rules are unchanged. The authors authorised versioned Figure 3 and ED8 reference updates to the public-CSV rendering and an ED2 aggregate-precision correction. The old references, old ED2 public CSVs and validation evidence are retained privately in the build archive. No other reference-update permission is implied.
